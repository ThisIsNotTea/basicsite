There is no single way to get involved. Some neighbors enjoy helping welcome people at
meetings; others lend a hand with communications, research, event planning, or community
service.

Start with a local gathering, meet the volunteers, and find a role that fits your
interests and schedule. See our [upcoming events](/events/) and [volunteer page](/get-involved/).
